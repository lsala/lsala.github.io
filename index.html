Research scientist · Applied mathematics

Mathematical modelling and learning for biological digital twins.

I develop mechanistic and data-informed mathematical models for complex biological systems. My research combines digital twins with inverse problems, reduced-order modelling and physics-informed neural networks to integrate experimental data, uncover hidden dynamics and deliver efficient predictions.
